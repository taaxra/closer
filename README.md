# Closer

A question game that gets your group past small talk. One phone, a few friends, and questions that go a little deeper each round.

**Play:** [taaxra.github.io/closer](https://taaxra.github.io/closer/)

## How to play

1. **Add the players.** Type in everyone's name. The app picks who answers each card, so everyone takes turns.
2. **Choose how deep you want to go.**
   - **Slow build:** starts light and ends deep. A good default.
   - **Warm-up:** light and fun. Good for new groups.
   - **Personal:** for friends who already know each other a bit.
   - **Deep:** for close friends and late nights.
3. **Pick how many questions:** 10, 20, 30, 40 or 50.
4. **Pass the phone around.** Read the card out loud. The person whose name is on it answers, and anyone can jump in after.
5. **Swipe or tap Next** for the next card. Not feeling a question? Tap **Skip**, no explanation needed.

## A few house rules

- There are no wrong answers.
- Listen more than you talk. Follow-up questions are encouraged.
- Skipping is always fine.
- What's said at the table stays at the table.

## Good to know

- 294 questions in three levels: Warm-up, Personal and Deep.
- Your phone remembers which questions you've already played and shows new ones first.
- Add it to your home screen to play it like an app, even offline: in Safari tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
